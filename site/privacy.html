Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 16, 2026

Information We Collect

TrainTrack collects the information you provide when you use the app:

  • Account information: email address, username, and display name
  • Profile picture, if you choose to upload one
  • Workout data: exercises, sets, weights, reps, durations, and dates
  • Body metrics: weight entries and notes
  • Goals, templates, custom exercises, and personal records
  • Social activity: friends, friend requests, shared workouts, likes, and comments
  • Reports and blocks you make, and feedback you send (with your app version and device type)

We do not use advertising, analytics, or tracking services, and we do not collect your location.

How We Use Your Information

  • Provide the TrainTrack service and sync your data across devices
  • Show your shared workouts and profile to your friends
  • Send reminders you turn on (these are scheduled on your device)
  • Review reports and keep the community safe
  • Respond to your feedback and improve the app

What Other Users Can See

  • Signed-in users can find you by username and see your display name, profile picture, week streak, and total workout count.
  • Only your friends can see workouts you share and comments on them. You can turn off workout sharing in your profile.
  • Your email address, weight entries, and unshared workouts are never visible to other users.

Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored on your device and in our cloud database, hosted by Supabase. Data is encrypted in transit, and access is restricted so users can only read data they are permitted to see.

Progress photos attached to weight entries stay on your device and are not uploaded. Profile pictures are uploaded so your friends can see them.

Information Sharing

We do not sell or rent your personal information. We share it only:

  • With other users, as described above
  • With Supabase, which stores data on our behalf
  • When required by law, or to protect the safety of our users

Your Choices and Rights

  • Export your data from Settings > Export Workout Data
  • Edit your username, display name, and profile picture at any time
  • Turn notifications on or off in Settings
  • Block users and manage blocked users in Settings
  • Permanently delete your account and all associated data from Settings > Delete Account. Deletion takes effect immediately and cannot be undone.

Children's Privacy

TrainTrack is not intended for children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has created an account, please email traintrack.app.support@gmail.com and we will delete it.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. We will post the updated policy in the app and update the “Last updated” date above.

Contact Us

Questions or requests about your privacy? Email traintrack.app.support@gmail.com, or use Settings > Send Feedback in the app and choose “Account / Privacy”.
