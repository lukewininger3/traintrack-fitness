Support

Need help with TrainTrack, found a bug, or want to report a user? We're here to help.

Contact us

Email traintrack.app.support@gmail.com. We usually reply within 1–2 days.

You can also send feedback from inside the app: Settings > Send Feedback.

Report a user or comment

Open the user's profile and tap ⋯ > Report User, or press and hold a comment and choose Report Comment. We review every report within 24 hours. You can also block a user from the same menus, and manage blocked users in Settings > Blocked Users.

Delete your account

Go to Settings > Delete Account. This permanently deletes your account and all of your data right away. If you can't sign in, email us from the address on your account and we'll delete it for you.

Export your data

Go to Settings > Export Workout Data.

Forgot your password?

On the sign-in screen, tap Forgot Password? and we'll email you a reset link.
